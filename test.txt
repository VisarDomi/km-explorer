Tester prerequisites:

- Run on an iPhone with Safari foregrounded.
- The universal userscript-ios-test debugger must be enabled and granted access
  to `ytboob.com`.
- Disable other installed copies of km-explorer while the suite injects the
  locally built userscript.
- The live provider must return enough entries to exercise multiple provider
  and client pages.
- Tests that change Favorites must snapshot and restore the original Favorites
  store.

Frozen critical behavior:

1. Favorites home
- Opening `https://ytboob.com/` replaces the provider page with km-explorer.
- `/` is Favorites and occupies the page-0 position in app navigation.
- Every favorite is rendered in one grid, newest first.
- Favorites is not divided into pages.
- Toggling a card's Favorite control immediately updates the Favorites store.
- Returning to `/` reflects the updated complete Favorites collection.

2. Provider and client pagination
- `/page/2/` opens client listing page 1.
- One client listing page contains up to 600 provider videos in provider order.
- The provider page in the URL determines the initial video position within
  its client page.
- Opening another provider URL mapped to the same client page renders the same
  client-page data and positions the corresponding video.
- The listing pagination appears above and below the video grid.
- Favorites appears in the page-0 position before the numbered client pages.
- Selecting page 0 opens `/`.
- Selecting a numbered client page opens the first provider URL represented by
  that client page.
- The active client page, provider URL, rendered video range, and initial
  position agree.

3. Listing position
- On `scrollend` + 100 ms, the current listing position is saved.
- Selecting a ready card leaves the listing through a real navigation.
- One Safari Back swipe restores the listing from bfcache.
- The restored document retains its DOM, active client page, ready-card state,
  and exact viewport position.

4. Card detail cache
- Each card resolves the detail data and media URL required to become ready.
- A card with cached detail data becomes ready without refetching that detail.
- Background card processing skips cards that are already ready.
- Repeated worker starts do not duplicate completed work or change a ready
  card's result.
- When a Favorites or listing page returns from bfcache, its card worker starts
  again from the first card.
- The restarted worker checks IndexedDB in card order, immediately restores
  cached cards, and resumes provider fetching at the first missing detail.

5. Opening a video
- Selecting a ready card copies its media URL during the activating gesture.
- The card then hard-navigates to that video's existing provider URL.
- No km-explorer-specific video URL is invented.
- A provider video URL is recognized and replaced by the km-explorer video
  page.
- The original Favorites or listing document remains available to Safari's
  bfcache.

6. Video page
- The selected video is displayed full width at the top of the page.
- The video has no native controls.
- The video starts muted.
- Precise current-time and duration numbers are displayed directly below the
  video.
- A seekable progress bar appears below the time display and tracks playback.
- A one-finger horizontal swipe on the video scrubs by 60 seconds per viewport
  width and resumes playback on release.
- Vertical movement, multi-touch, and a swipe beginning in Safari's 28px
  leading-edge Back area remain browser-owned.
- Icon-only play/pause and mute/unmute controls appear below the progress bar.
- The page does not automatically scroll after takeover or data loading.
- Safari attempts to play the selected media.
- The navigation-time media copy happens regardless of whether Safari
  successfully plays the video.
- Playback failure does not prevent the rest of the video page from becoming
  usable.

7. Actor-video grid
- Every video has exactly one actor for application purposes.
- The video page renders that actor's video entries below the player controls.
- No actor selector, actor tabs, or actor-navigation link is required.
- Actor-video cards use the same readiness and Favorite behavior as other
  video cards.
- The currently displayed video is highlighted in the actor grid.
- The highlighted current-video card cannot copy or navigate to itself.
- The currently displayed video and its actor-video grid are derived from the
  provider video URL and provider data.

8. Video replacement and history
- Selecting a ready actor-video card copies its media URL during the activating
  gesture.
- It opens the selected card's existing provider video URL with
  `location.replace()`.
- The replacement page displays the newly selected video and its actor-video
  grid.
- Further actor-video selections continue replacing the current video history
  entry.
- Video-to-video navigation does not add Back steps.
- One Safari Back swipe from any replacement video returns directly to the
  original Favorites or listing page.
- The originating page is restored from bfcache with its previous viewport and
  card state.
- Its idempotent card worker restarts after restoration.

9. IndexedDB behavior
- Cached video summaries, details, media URLs, actor results, and Favorites are
  reused when available.
- Cached data makes the corresponding UI usable before missing background work
  finishes.
- Missing data is fetched and stored without duplicating completed card work.
- Reloading an application route reconstructs the correct UI from cached and
  newly fetched provider data.

10. Provider boundary
- Shared routing and UI do not import ytboob-specific operations directly.
- The active provider recognizes Favorites, listing, video, and actor data
  routes.
- The provider owns mapping between provider pages and client pages.
- The provider owns listing queries, video-detail extraction, actor-video
  queries, thumbnails, existing provider URLs, and media-source extraction.
- Shared Favorites, listing, video, card-cache, navigation, and bfcache behavior
  consume the provider interface.
